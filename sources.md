# Sources

All 552 documents are publicly available PDFs about Mandera County, Kenya. They were downloaded by hand before 4 Nov 2026 and are kept outside the repo (`CORPUS_DIR`, here `~/Downloads/Mandera`). They are never committed. Each file, with its type and year, is listed in `inventory.csv`.

**Status:** *confirmed* means a site's address is printed in the documents themselves. *Inferred* means the publisher is guessed from the file name or document type. Inferred rows should be checked before the README is published.

## Humanitarian reports (50)

Situation reports, emergency plans of action, rapid assessments, needs assessments and cash programme factsheets, 2013–2026. Publishers include UN OCHA, IFRC / Kenya Red Cross (DREF), REACH, UNICEF, IOM and NGO consortia.

- **Where from:** reliefweb.int. The `YYYY-MM-DD Title.pdf` naming (38 files) matches ReliefWeb's report pages, and reliefweb.int and unocha.org are printed in some files.
- **Status:** partly confirmed.

## Research (118)

Master's theses and journal articles on education, health, security, water and governance in Mandera, plus a few NGO studies (UNDP, ACDI/VOCA, MMC).

- **Where from:** university repositories (Africa Nazarene University and others) and open-access journals (AJOL, IOSR, IJHSS and others, with DOI links printed).
- **Status:** confirmed for some.
